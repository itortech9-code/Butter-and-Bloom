# Butter-and-Bloom
Butter & Bloom 🌸🧈

A little sweetness. A little beauty. A lot of love.

Welcome to the Butter & Bloom website repository — a digital home for a brand that celebrates the beauty of flowers and the joy of freshly baked treats.

🌷 About the Project

Butter & Bloom is a modern, elegant, and user-friendly website designed to showcase the brand’s products, share its story, and help customers explore available offerings.

The website aims to deliver a beautiful browsing experience with a clean layout, warm visual identity, and responsive design across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

✨ Features

* Elegant Design: A beautiful, brand-focused interface.
* Responsive Layout: Designed for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
* Product Showcase: Display baked goods, floral arrangements, or other brand offerings.
* About Section: Introduce the brand and its story.
* Contact Information: Make it easy for customers to get in touch.
* Easy Navigation: Help visitors find what they need quickly.
